ABOUT AGO

Proudly Ugandan. Built for service, innovation and sustainable growth.

Al-Haqq Global Oils (Uganda) Limited is a private limited liability company established in July 2022; the business idea was conceived in 2015.

COMPANY SUMMARY

An indigenous service provider to Uganda's oil and gas economy.

AGO was incorporated to undertake fuel wholesale and retail network services, pipeline services, procurement and transport logistics. The company also engages in marketing, supply and trading of crude oil and petroleum products in neighbouring South Sudan and DR Congo.

The company has diversified into construction and maintenance, water and construction materials, sports materials, general merchandise and other support services.
